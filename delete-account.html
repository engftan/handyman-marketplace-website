Request Account Deletion

Handyman Marketplace

Handyman Marketplace users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Handyman Marketplace account.
  2. Use the subject line: Handyman Marketplace Account Deletion Request.
  3. State clearly that you want your Handyman Marketplace account deleted.
Request Account Deletion

If your account was created using a mobile phone number rather than an email address, include the mobile number associated with the account, including the country code.

What will be deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, we will delete or anonymise, where applicable:

  • Your Handyman Marketplace user account and profile information.
  • Account identifiers such as the email address or mobile number associated with the account.
  • Saved provider information and personal notes associated with your account.
  • Community content associated with your account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
  • Other personal information associated with your account that is no longer required to operate the service or meet legal obligations.

Information that may be retained

Certain limited records may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, transaction, dispute-resolution or regulatory purposes. Such information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing your request

We may need to verify that the deletion request was made by the account holder before processing it.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. After the account is deleted, access to Premium entitlements, AI credits, saved providers and other account-linked information may no longer be available.
